# Debrief SLA Policy

## Scope

This SLA applies to all interview debriefs for hiring loops.

## Due Time

- Owner: the interviewer who ran the session.
- Debrief due: same calendar day as interview end, by `23:59:59 UTC`.

## Escalation Path

1. At +0 hours past due: reminder sent to interviewer.
2. At +4 hours past due: escalate to hiring manager.
3. At +24 hours past due: escalate to recruiting lead and QA for process incident tracking.

## Weekly Review Owner

- Primary owner: Recruiting Operations.
- QA owner: Founding QA Engineer validates weekly compliance report integrity.
