# Sourcing Review Agenda

## Objective

Review top-of-funnel health and confirm sourcing actions for priority roles.

## Agenda (30 min)

- Pipeline snapshot by role and stage (10 min)
- Top candidate outreach and response-rate review (10 min)
- Owner-based action list with due dates (10 min)

## Required Inputs

- Current candidate funnel counts by stage
- Outreach conversion metrics for prior week
- Open blocker list for sourcing capacity
