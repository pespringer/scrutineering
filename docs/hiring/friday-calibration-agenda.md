# Friday Calibration Agenda

## Objective

Finalize candidate disposition decisions and lock next-week interview/offer priorities.

## Agenda (45 min)

- Candidate packet review: scorecards + risks (20 min)
- Debrief SLA exceptions and corrective actions (10 min)
- Offer-close plan and owner confirmation (10 min)
- Decisions recap and approvals (5 min)

## Required Inputs

- Completed scorecards and interview notes
- Debrief compliance report for current week
- Compensation and leveling recommendation where applicable
