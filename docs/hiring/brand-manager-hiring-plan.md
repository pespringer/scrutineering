# Brand Manager — Hiring Plan & Role Definition

## Role Overview

**Title:** Brand Manager  
**Reports to:** CMO (Chief Marketing Officer)  
**Level:** Mid-to-Senior Individual Contributor  
**Location:** Remote-first, aligned with core working hours  

## Mission

The Brand Manager will own the go-to-market brand strategy for Scrutineering (Container Sentinel). They will work with the CMO and CEO to define positioning, messaging, and marketing channels that efficiently drive awareness, trial, and adoption of our automated container vulnerability scanning platform.

## Core Responsibilities

1. **Brand Strategy & Positioning**
   - Define and maintain the brand identity for Scrutineering
   - Develop positioning statements and value propositions for target personas (DevSecOps leaders, platform engineers, security architects)
   - Ensure consistent brand voice across all marketing touchpoints

2. **Go-To-Market Marketing**
   - Design and execute marketing campaigns across digital channels (content, social, email, events)
   - Build demand generation funnels from awareness → trial → conversion
   - Create case studies, webinars, and thought leadership content in partnership with engineering

3. **Market Intelligence**
   - Monitor competitive landscape (Aqua Security, Sysdig, Prisma Cloud, Trivy ecosystem)
   - Track market trends in container security, DevSecOps, and cloud-native infrastructure
   - Provide regular competitive analysis to product and executive teams

4. **Community & Ecosystem**
   - Engage with open-source communities (Trivy, Grype, Kubernetes, OWASP)
   - Build relationships with security influencers and podcast hosts
   - Represent Scrutineering at conferences (KubeCon, DEF CON, RSA, Black Hat)

5. **Marketing Operations**
   - Define marketing KPIs and report on campaign performance
   - Manage marketing technology stack (CMS, email platform, analytics)
   - Coordinate with the CMO on budget allocation and ROI tracking

## Must-Have Qualifications

- 4+ years of B2B SaaS marketing experience, preferably in security/infrastructure/DevOps
- Demonstrated ability to develop brand strategy and execute multi-channel campaigns
- Understanding of the DevSecOps ecosystem — knows what a CVE is, understands containers, CI/CD
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills — can translate technical value into business outcomes
- Data-driven mindset — comfortable with analytics, A/B testing, and attribution modeling
- Experience working in a startup or small team environment (resourcefulness required)

## Nice-to-Have Qualifications

- Experience marketing developer tools or open-source products
- Background in cybersecurity or DevOps (former practitioner preferred)
- Event management experience (conference booths, speaking coordination)
- Familiarity with product-led growth (PLG) and developer marketing tactics
- Existing network in the DevSecOps or cloud-native security community

## Interview Process

| Stage | Format | Duration | Interviewer(s) | Focus |
|-------|--------|----------|----------------|-------|
| 1 | Recruiter screen | 30 min | CMO (when hired) or CEO | Background check, role alignment, compensation |
| 2 | Brand strategy exercise | 60 min | CMO + CEO | Candidate presents a go-to-market brand plan for a hypothetical DevSecOps product |
| 3 | Writing assessment | Take-home (48h) | CMO | Write a blog post explaining container security scanning to a non-technical audience |
| 4 | Culture & values fit | 30 min | CEO | Startup mindset, communication style, working preferences |

## Evaluation Rubric

| Competency | Weight | Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| Brand/Marketing Strategy | 30% | Clear thinking, audience understanding, channel expertise |
| DevSecOps/Technical Fluency | 25% | Understands the market, can speak to technical buyers |
| Communication Skills | 20% | Clear, concise, persuasive writing and speaking |
| Data/Analytical Ability | 15% | Uses metrics to drive decisions, understands attribution |
| Startup Fit | 10% | Self-motivated, resourceful, comfortable with ambiguity |

**Scoring:** Each competency scored 1-5. Minimum passing: 3.5 average with no individual score below 3.

## Compensation Range

- **Base salary:** $90,000 - $130,000 (depending on experience and location)
- **Equity:** 0.1% - 0.3% (subject to equity pool availability)
- **Benefits:** Standard startup package (health, PTO, remote equipment, conference budget)

| Milestone | Deliverable |

| Day 1-30 | Completed brand audit, competitive landscape report, and initial positioning document |
| Day 31-60 | Launched first marketing campaign (content + social), established KPI dashboard |

## Sourcing Channels

1. **LinkedIn Recruiter** — Search for "Brand Manager" + "SaaS" + "security" or "infrastructure"
2. **AngelList / Wellfound** — Startup-focused job board, attracts candidates who want meaningful impact
3. **DevRel/SecOps communities** — Slack groups, Discord, Reddit (r/devsecops, r/kubernetes)
4. **Conference networking** — KubeCon, RSA, DEF CON attendee lists
5. **Referrals** — Ask CMO and any existing team members for referrals
6. **Content marketing inbound** — Publish brand/role content to attract passive candidates

## Reporting Cadence

- **Weekly:** 1:1 with CMO
- **Bi-weekly:** Marketing status update to CEO
- **Monthly:** Campaign performance review with metrics dashboard
- **Quarterly:** Strategy review and next-quarter planning
